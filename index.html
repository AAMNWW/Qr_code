The Problem

Why Paper Registers Don't Work Well

Taking attendance the old way, by calling names and ticking a register, causes lots of problems.

⏳

Wastes Time

Calling out every name can take several minutes from each lesson, every day.

🙋

Proxy Attendance

A friend can answer "present" for someone who isn't actually in class.

✏️

Human Errors

Wrong ticks, missed names and messy handwriting lead to wrong records.

📚

Hard to Manage

Registers can be lost or damaged, and working out percentages by hand is slow.

Our Solution

What is a QR Attendance System?

A smart way to take attendance using QR codes and a camera.

A QR code based attendance system gives every student a unique QR code, usually printed on their ID card. The QR code holds the student's ID number.

When the student arrives, the teacher scans the code with a phone, tablet or webcam. The system reads the ID, finds the student in the database, and automatically marks them present with the exact date and time.

At the end of the day, the school gets a complete digital attendance report, with no paper and no adding up by hand.

💡
Quick recap: what is a QR code? QR stands for Quick Response. It's a square barcode invented in Japan in 1994 by Denso Wave. Unlike a normal barcode, it stores information both across and down, so it holds much more data and can be scanned in under a second.
How it Works

Four Simple Steps

From signing up students to downloading the final report.

📝1

Register Students

Each student's name and class is saved in the system and given a unique ID number.

🪪2

Generate QR Codes

The system creates a QR code for every ID, printed on the student's ID card.

📷3

Scan in Class

The teacher scans each card. The system marks the student present with the time.

📊4

Get Reports

Attendance is saved instantly. Teachers can view and download daily reports.

Method A

🪪 Student ID Cards

Each student has their own QR code. The teacher's device scans it.

  • Works with just one phone or laptop
  • Students don't need their own phones
  • Best for schools (this is what our demo uses)
Method B

📱 Class QR Code

The teacher shows one QR code on the board. Students scan it with their own phones.

  • Very fast for big classes and lectures
  • The code changes every few seconds to stop sharing
  • Common in colleges, offices and events

👍 Advantages

  • ✓ Saves lesson time
  • ✓ Records the exact time each student arrives
  • ✓ Reduces proxy (fake) attendance
  • ✓ No paper, so it's eco-friendly
  • ✓ Low cost: QR codes are free to make
  • ✓ Instant reports for teachers and parents

👎 Disadvantages

  • ✕ Needs a working phone, camera or scanner
  • ✕ Online systems need internet and power
  • ✕ Students may forget or lose their cards
  • ✕ A card could still be given to a friend
  • ✕ Student data must be kept private and secure
Security

How to Stop Cheating

What if a student shares their QR code with a friend? Real systems use extra checks to make sure the right person is marked present.

🔄
Dynamic QR codesThe class QR code changes every few seconds, so a photo of it quickly stops working.
⏰
Time limitsScanning only works during the first few minutes of the lesson.
📍
Location checkThe phone's GPS confirms the student is really at school.
🧑‍💼
Photo or face checkThe system shows the student's photo, or uses face recognition, to confirm it's them.
👀
Teacher supervisionThe teacher watches the scans and does a quick head count.
